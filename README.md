# MAHAMAYA CLINIC OS — Foundation V1.0

Integrated clinic operations app for:
- Finance and daily audit
- Quick patient billing
- Separate Ayurveda and Allopathic medicine inventory
- Injection/IV/procedure inventory
- Dressing/Suturing/POP inventory
- Panchakarma materials
- Orthopedic supports
- Printing & stationery
- General consumables
- Vendors / parties
- Staff & salary
- Monthly Swarnaprashan finance
- Free/sponsored camp cost centres
- Clinical equipment / furniture / interior assets
- Repair, maintenance, AMC and calibration
- Daily closing and reconciliation
- Monthly reports

## Important
This is a local-first foundation build. Data is stored in the browser's localStorage.
Use **Backup JSON** regularly.

Cloud/Firebase sync, authentication, permissions, true double-entry accounting,
advanced purchase orders, batch movement ledgers, direct payment integrations,
and regulatory-grade accounting are intentionally NOT enabled in this first build.

## Recommended first test
1. Add 1 staff member
2. Add 1 Ayurveda stock item
3. Add 1 Allopathic stock item
4. Create 1 patient bill
