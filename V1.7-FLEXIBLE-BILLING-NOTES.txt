MAHAMAYA CLINIC OS V1.7 — FLEXIBLE COMPASSIONATE BILLING
Build: 2026-08-12 23:56 IST

What changed
• Quick Billing now uses service-specific approved minimum–maximum ranges.
• Any whole-rupee rate within the configured range can be selected.
• Quick rate chips remain available for fast billing.
• ₹0 is permitted only for services whose range includes ₹0 (Consultation / Follow-up Seva).
• Rate is automatically clamped if a value outside the approved range is entered.
• Optional Rate Decision / Concession Note is stored with the bill.
• Pathology: editable test name + custom amount.
• Medicines: total medicine/pharmacy amount.
• Previous bills, finance records, login, Firebase/cloud and backup architecture preserved.

Examples
Ceftriaxone 1 g Direct IV: minimum ₹100, maximum ₹200.
You may bill ₹100, ₹120, ₹150, ₹180 or ₹200 — but not below ₹100 or above ₹200.

Consultation: ₹0–₹200.
Follow-up: ₹0–₹100.
