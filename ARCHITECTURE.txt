MAHAMAYA CLINIC OS — ARCHITECTURE NOTES

Core verticals:
1. OPD / Billing
2. Ayurveda / Panchakarma
3. Orthopedic / Spine
4. Allopathic Medicines
5. Finance / Audit
6. Procurement / Vendors
7. Inventory / Assets
8. Staff / Payroll
9. Swarnaprashan
10. Camps / Seva

Inventory is deliberately split into:
- Ayurveda Medicines
- Allopathic Medicines
- Injection/IV & Procedure
- Dressing/Suturing/POP
- Panchakarma Materials
- Orthopedic Supports
- Printing & Stationery
- General Consumables

Asset / equipment is deliberately separate from consumable inventory:
- Ayurvedic Clinical Equipment
- Modern/Allopathic Clinical Equipment
- Reusable Instruments
- IT & Electronics
- Furniture & Fixtures
- Interior / Decorative
- Signage / Branding
- Electrical / Utility Assets

Future phases:
V1.1: Purchase invoices + stock movements + vendor payment ledger
V1.2: Patient accounts + multi-service bills + PDF receipts
V1.3: Role-based login + cloud sync + multi-device
V1.4: Audit hardening + immutable corrections + approval workflows
V1.5: Advanced analytics + reorder + expiry + budget + cash-flow forecasting
